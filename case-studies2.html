Enabling Business Continuity During COVID-19

Equipping thousands of employees for remote work during an unprecedented disruption.

Executive Summary

When COVID-19 brought workplaces to a standstill, organisations faced an urgent challenge — enabling employees to work effectively from home. Amid severe supply-chain and logistical constraints, a leading telecom company turned to Saitronics to rapidly equip its workforce with essential technology accessories.

Saitronics supplied 25,000+ IT accessories, including headsets, keyboard-mouse combos, chargers, and adapters, while managing the distribution of these products directly to employees' homes across the country.

The Challenge

The requirement went beyond simply sourcing products. Thousands of employees needed essential workplace accessories at a time when conventional supply and distribution channels were severely constrained. The client needed a partner capable of managing large-scale procurement, fulfilment and direct-to-home delivery under unprecedented circumstances.

The Saitronics Solution

Saitronics mobilised its procurement and fulfilment capabilities to source the required equipment despite severe availability constraints and manage its distribution at scale.

Rather than relying on centralised corporate delivery, we enabled direct-to-employee fulfilment, ensuring the required technology reached individual homes and supported the rapid transition to remote working.

The Outcome

25,000+ Units Delivered

Essential technology supplied at significant scale.

Direct-to-Home Delivery

Equipment delivered directly to employees across the country.

Business Continuity Enabled

Helped thousands of employees establish productive remote work environments.

End-to-End Execution

From sourcing and fulfilment to last-mile delivery, Saitronics managed the entire process.

When Circumstances Change Overnight, So Do We

When circumstances changed overnight, Saitronics adapted just as quickly — helping keep thousands of employees equipped, connected and ready to work.
