Our Story

From a single room to a Pan-India technology partner.

Saitronics began in 1994 as a one-person venture, working out of a single room with a simple belief: that lasting businesses are built on trust, not transactions. There was no large team, no ready client list — only a founder's instinct for what businesses actually needed from their technology, and the discipline to deliver it properly, every time.

That instinct became a method. Instead of just supplying devices, we took ownership of the whole environment — specifying it, deploying it and standing behind it long after the invoice was settled. Over three decades, this approach carried Saitronics from a small technology supplier to a trusted partner for over 700 enterprise, institutional and government clients across India.

Today, that same principle shapes everything we do — including Cytomec, our own structured cabling brand, built to bring the same direct accountability to the infrastructure layer that our clients have relied on us for since day one.
